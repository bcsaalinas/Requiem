# Tutorial audio sources

- `chest_latch_and_lid.wav` — short metal latch and wooden lid knock edited from `metal_hit_01.wav` and `door_knock_01.wav` in the bundled Horror Jam Kit (CC0 source recordings).

- `prayer_female.mp3` (now the sound set `viv/prayer_voice`, in `assets/audio/viv/prayer_voice/`) — “whisper ave maria in Swedish for georden_west” by drotzruhn, [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/drotzruhn/sounds/448704/), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Female whispered prayer in Swedish; 12-second excerpt, normalized, filtered and faded. Author credited in game.
- `chest_lid.mp3` — “Wooden Chest Lid Open” by WakuWakuWakuWaku, CC0, [SFX Free](https://sfxfree.com/tag/chest), source fs-422975. Normalized for a clear hinge and latch sound.

- `stone_throw.wav` — `snd_throw1.wav` by Spring Spring, CC0, from [Various Sound Effects](https://opengameart.org/content/various-sound-effects-0). Trimmed and filtered for the stone release.
- `battery_insert.wav` — by pauliuw, CC0, from [Gun reload, lock or click sound](https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-lock-or-click-sound). Filtered for flashlight battery insertion.
- `pickup_hand_1.wav` through `pickup_hand_4.wav` — edits from “Fabric Rustling” by Iochi Glaucus, CC0, [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/fabric-rustling). Trimmed and filtered into short soft foley variations.
- `chest_open.mp3` — “Wooden Chest Opening” by spookymodem, CC0 1.0, [SFX Free](https://sfxfree.com/sound/fs-202092-wooden-chest-opening). This replaces the generic door creak with a chest lid opening and settling thud.
- `prayer_ritual.mp3` — 12-second cut of “Melkite Priest Prayer” by jgntrs, CC0 1.0, [SFX Free](https://sfxfree.com/sound/fs-193355-melkite-priest-prayer). Trimmed to the altar's 12-second ritual and faded at the end.
