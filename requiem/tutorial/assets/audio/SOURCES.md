# Tutorial audio sources

- `stone_throw.wav` — `snd_throw1.wav` by Spring Spring, CC0, from [Various Sound Effects](https://opengameart.org/content/various-sound-effects-0). Trimmed and filtered for the stone release.
- `battery_insert.wav` — by pauliuw, CC0, from [Gun reload, lock or click sound](https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-lock-or-click-sound). Filtered for flashlight battery insertion.
- `pickup_hand_1.wav` through `pickup_hand_4.wav` — edits from “Fabric Rustling” by Iochi Glaucus, CC0, [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/fabric-rustling). Trimmed and filtered into short soft foley variations.
