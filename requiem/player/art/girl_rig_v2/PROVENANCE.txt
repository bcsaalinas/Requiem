Requiem girl rig v2

Art direction reconstruction of the generation brief (not a verbatim API transcript):
Create one modular 3x3 texture-parts sheet of a single practical young woman survivor in a muted weathered olive-charcoal jacket, ivory shirt, dark trousers, boots, brown crown and ponytail, with separate unlit flashlight. Steep overhead game perspective, realistic grounded proportions, textured surfaces, consistent soft neutral shading, no scene shadow or emitted light. Cells: torso; crown/ponytail; left upper arm; left forearm/hand; right upper arm; right forearm/hand; left trouser/boot; right trouser/boot; flashlight.
