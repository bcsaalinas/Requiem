Requiem girl rig v2

Art direction reconstruction of the generation brief (not a verbatim API transcript):
Create one modular 3x3 texture-parts sheet of a single practical young woman survivor in a muted weathered olive-charcoal jacket, ivory shirt, dark trousers, boots, brown crown and ponytail, with separate unlit flashlight. Steep overhead game perspective, realistic grounded proportions, textured surfaces, consistent soft neutral shading, no scene shadow or emitted light. Cells: torso; crown/ponytail; left upper arm; left forearm/hand; right upper arm; right forearm/hand; left trouser/boot; right trouser/boot; flashlight.

Original generated source: tools/character_rig/source_parts.png
SHA256: 281072703372ea992e80b5961d1301f7b5101bd49eaf022d221d5ce88d2f9cf3

Bake: bake_rig.py composes only source pixels with fixed component transforms.
No third-party model or purchased asset was used.
See tools/character_rig/manifest.json for complete pivot, timing, stance and frame information.

Equipped action layers: export_action_layers.py composes the same source parts.
grip_rig.py crops the relaxed right hand out of the equipped sleeve and constructs
a compact hand/flashlight attachment. Its wrist, palm and torch rotate together;
neutral forearm art is unchanged. No new generated artwork is used.
gait_rig.py bounds lateral reach, retains anatomical left/right foot lanes, and
exports independent leg sheets with contact, joint and rigid boot landmarks.
Four staged poses per 45-degree idle turn transfer support between the feet.
The original girl_atlas.png / girl_frames.tres remain available unchanged.
The original base locomotion exports use a 0.90-second walk/sprint cycle;
the live renderer selects the separate distance-driven sprint below.
See tools/character_rig/action_layers_manifest.json and generated action_layer_anchors.gd for the
registered upper, lower, turn and grip coordinates used by the live renderer.

Authoring scripts and source sheet: tools/character_rig/ (outside the Godot project).

Hold Your Breath upper-body track: tools/character_rig/breath_rig.py constructs
seven tracks (inhale, hold, strain, release, gasp, recovery, loud release) in
neutral and five equipped aim modes from the same original cutouts. The 222
frames interpolate the individual cutout transforms. Shoulder/chest/head and free
arm gestures carry the breath animation. The equipped arm keeps its gentle
12-degree elbow and rigid hand/torch attachment. No new generated art is used.
The original locomotion exports and planted-foot geometry are unchanged.
See tools/character_rig/breath_layers_manifest.json and breath_layer_anchors.gd.

Throw 03 upper-body track: tools/character_rig/throw_rig.py uses the same original
cutouts to bake 12 windup and 10 follow-through poses in six equipment/aim modes.
The anatomical left arm gathers, draws back, releases and settles. Its palm socket
is registered to the transformed forearm's source pixels. The equipped right arm,
single hand/torch attachment and original leg assets retain their existing geometry.
All six modes have exact neutral endpoints and identical release/follow-through
seams. See throw_layers_manifest.json and throw_layer_anchors.gd.

Prayer 04 upper-body track: tools/character_rig/prayer_rig.py composes the same
original cutouts into 8 entry, 8 sustained-loop and 8 exit poses per mode, six
modes / 144 frames. A readable kneeling bow and free-palm gathering preserve the
existing equipped elbow and single hand/torch attachment. Two new 24-frame
leg sheets fold original trouser and boot cutouts behind the back; the original
locomotion sheets and support solver remain unchanged.
Entry and exit reverse exactly; neutral endpoints and held seams match.
See prayer_layers_manifest.json and prayer_layer_anchors.gd.

Dedicated sprint: tools/character_rig/sprint_rig.py deterministically composes
only the same original source cutouts. Six equipment/aim modes contain eight
upper poses each (48 frames, sprint_upper_layers.png, 2048 x 1536). Each
independent sprint_left_leg_layers.png / sprint_right_leg_layers.png sheet
contains eight relative directions x eight poses (64 frames, 2048 x 2048).
All cells retain the 256 x 256 canvas, (128,128) pivot and 0.5 world scale.

The nominal cycle is 0.60 seconds at the unchanged 172.8 world pixels/second,
covering 103.68 pixels per full stride. PlayerMotion advances sprint sprite
phase from actual displacement / stride distance; CharacterAnimation preserves
phase through walk/sprint changes. Walking and held-breath poses follow the existing
footstep clock; gameplay hearing cadence is unchanged. FootstepAudio now
plays short one-shots on the resolved contacts (tools/audio/README.md).

A stronger lean, tucked alternating arm drive and restrained head/shoulder
rhythm distinguish sprint. Free upper/forearm bones keep 18-pixel projected
lengths; equipped bones remain 16 pixels with the same 12-degree elbow and
single rigid hand/torch attachment. Boots stay rigid at 18 x 24 source pixels,
in anatomical left/right lanes with at most seven pixels of lateral excursion
and unchanged three-degree toe splay. Bounded projected knees fold on recovery.
Contact, swing progress and passing frames 2/6 feed the existing support solver.
Original source pixels and all previous animation exports are preserved.

From tools/character_rig, bake with:
python sprint_rig.py --review-dir ../../requiem/testing/captures/sprint_review
Validate geometry without rewriting exports with:
python sprint_rig.py --validate-only
See sprint_layers_manifest.json and sprint_layer_anchors.gd for source hash,
joint/boot geometry, runtime sockets, cycle duration and stride distance.
